Architecture Overview

The architectural style and a short narrative of how the components work together (Matt).

Component Summary

IDComponentResponsibility
C1User InterfaceScreens for accounts, games, characters, parties, and the consolidated progression view.
C2Game Data AdapterLoads and validates each game's definition data (level caps, EXP curves, stats, rewards, unlocks) and exposes it to the rest of the system in one common format.
C3Stat CalculatorComputes character stats from base values, item stats, and multipliers.
C4Progression EngineTracks EXP and levels, computes next-level and target-level requirements, including materials.
C5Rewards & UnlocksDetermines rewards, items, equipment, and locations unlocked at each level.
C6DatabaseStores accounts, characters, parties, progression records, and game definitions.
C7Account & SecuritySign-up, sign-in/out, authorization, encryption of sensitive data, and account deletion.
C8Party ManagerGroups characters into parties and builds party summaries.
