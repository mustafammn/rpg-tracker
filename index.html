Project Overview

Role-playing games (RPGs) reward players for leveling up their characters, but the information a player needs to plan that progress is scattered: how much EXP the next level takes, which materials it consumes, what stats, items, and locations it unlocks, and how all of this adds up across a whole party. Players often end up checking wikis, spreadsheets, and in-game menus just to decide what to work on next.

The RPG Character Progression Tracker gives players one place to track this. A user adds the supported games they play, enters their characters' current levels and EXP, and sees a consolidated view of what each character needs for the next level and what it will gain. Users can group characters into parties, plan toward a target level, and keep their data across sessions and devices. Each game's progression rules are stored as data, so new games can be added without changing the application.
