Customer and Intended Users

Customer: an online RPG community and game-guide website that commissions the tracker to offer to its members as a free companion tool. The customer wants one tool that covers many games, keeps users coming back to its site, and can add new games quickly as they are released.

Intended users: people who play role-playing video games (RPGs) from a preset list of supported games, and who want an easy way to track the progression requirements and rewards for their account, their characters, and their parties of grouped characters.

Functional Requirements

Each requirement has a unique identifier that is referenced on the design, traceability, and rationale pages. Sub-requirements are numbered under their parent, for example FR-4.1.

IDRequirement
FR-1The system must allow a user to create an account and to sign in and out.
FR-2The system must allow a user to add one or more supported games to their account and to create, edit, and delete multiple characters for each game.
FR-3The system must allow a user to manually enter and update a character's current level and EXP (and account-level progression, where the game defines it).
FR-4The system must present a single consolidated view per character that includes the items in FR-4.1 through FR-4.6.
FR-4.1EXP required for the next level and the user's progress toward it.
FR-4.2Level-up materials required for the next level.
FR-4.3Rewards granted at each level (e.g., in-game currency, stat increases, items).
FR-4.4A before-and-after comparison of stats for a level-up, showing the difference for each stat.
FR-4.5Equipment and items unlocked at each level.
FR-4.6Locations unlocked at each level.
FR-5The system must allow a user to group characters into one or more parties for games that support parties.
FR-6The system must display a party summary showing each member's level and next-level requirements.
FR-7The system must allow a user to select a target level and display the cumulative EXP, materials, and rewards from the current level to that target.
FR-8The system must store games as data that defines their progression mechanics (level caps, EXP curves, stats, rewards, unlock requirements), and must support multiple progression models, including level-based, account-wide, and character-specific.
FR-9The system must store all accounts, characters, parties, and progression records across sessions and devices, and must allow a user to permanently delete their account and all associated data.

Non-Functional Requirements

IDCategoryRequirement
NF-1AvailabilityThe system shall maintain at least 99% monthly availability, excluding scheduled maintenance.
NF-2PerformanceThe system shall support at least 200 concurrent active users while maintaining standard performance.
NF-3ScalabilityThe system shall support at least 5,000 registered users to account for growth across several RPG communities.
NF-4AccessibilityThe system shall meet the accessibility requirements in NF-4.1 through NF-4.4.
NF-4.1AccessibilityAll pages shall be usable with current versions of screen readers, with text alternatives for all non-text content such as charts, icons, and item images.
NF-4.2AccessibilityUsers across browsers shall be able to view and interact with the system.
NF-4.3AccessibilityUsers across devices shall be able to view and interact with the system.
NF-4.4AccessibilityText and interface elements shall meet minimum color-contrast ratios.
NF-5SecurityThe system shall meet the security requirements in NF-5.1 and NF-5.2.
NF-5.1SecuritySensitive user data shall be encrypted to protect account and progression information if the system is compromised.
NF-5.2SecurityThe system shall validate user profiles to prevent unauthorized users from viewing or modifying private character and progression data.
NF-6CapacityThe system shall support data storage and maintenance of at least 200 games to account for new RPG releases.
NF-7ExtensibilityThe system shall support the addition of new game information without requiring major changes to existing game data or application logic.
NF-8Data accuracyThe system shall regularly perform reference checks to ensure displayed progression information remains accurate.
NF-9Data integrityThe system shall maintain data integrity by preventing race conditions in database transactions.
NF-10RecoverabilityThe system shall maintain backups of game and user information to restore the system after data loss or corruption.

Example Target User

Erik, a casual RPG player getting serious

Erik is a 22-year-old graduate student in computer science who plays video games in his downtime. He has recently gotten into RPGs and wants to progress further so he can see more of their stories, but he is often unsure how to level up fastest and which levels unlock the areas he wants to explore.

Erik wants to:

  • Choose a game and enter his character information to see what he would gain by leveling up (FR-2, FR-3, FR-4).
  • Enter multiple characters and group them, since his favorite RPG has a party system (FR-5, FR-6).
  • See which materials a level-up needs, because some materials are much harder for him to get than others (FR-4.2, FR-7).
  • Update a character's level after leveling up and immediately see the new requirements and rewards (FR-3, FR-4).
  • Rely on the system to already know each game's requirements and rewards, without entering them himself (FR-8).
  • Delete his account if he stops playing, for example to start a PhD (FR-9).
